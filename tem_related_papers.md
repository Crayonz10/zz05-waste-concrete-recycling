# Related TEM Papers (Past Three Years)

## Paper 1: Yuan et al. (2025)
- **Title**: Exploring the Evolution of Collaborative Innovation in the Construction and Demolition Waste Management Industry
- **Journal**: IEEE Transactions on Engineering Management, 72, 2978-2994
- **DOI**: 10.1109/TEM.2025.3580426

**Relationship**: Shares the same application context—construction and demolition (C&D) waste management industry in China. However, Yuan et al. (2025) employ patent analysis and social network analysis at the industry level to examine collaborative innovation patterns, whereas our study develops a firm-level game-theoretic model to analyze operational decisions.

**Merits**: Provides empirical evidence on collaborative innovation stages and network characteristics in the C&D waste industry, identifying key technological shifts from recycled building materials to crushing technology.

---

## Our Paper's Distinctive Contributions

1. **Methodology**: We develop a two-stage biform game model analyzing both collection price competition (non-cooperative stage) and R&D investment cooperation (cooperative stage), providing analytical rather than empirical insights.

2. **Game-theoretic framework**: Our study extends the application of game theory to operational decisions in waste concrete recycling, whereas Yuan et al. (2025) focus on patent-based innovation analysis.

3. **Policy integration**: We incorporate the cap-and-trade mechanism into the operational model, evaluating how environmental policies affect firms' economic decisions and cooperation incentives.
